the app

type me it is a dictation tool for macos 26 or newer on apple silicon. you hold the dictation key (fn in the version from this site, ⌥ space in the mac app store version), speak, and the words are typed where your cursor is. transcription happens on your mac. the privacy page describes what the app stores and the servers it talks to.
