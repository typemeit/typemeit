# Model scout 2026-10-16

FluidAudio release check: nothing newer than 0.17.7. Versions 0.17.6 and 0.17.7 add text-to-speech only (Kokoro ANE, Paradee-8M), and every model the pinned 0.17.5 can load is already tried or in `seen.json`.

All three picks are older models. They count as new because their Open ASR Leaderboard rows are new to us. The leaderboard moved its table to `english_short_latest.csv`, and the rows endpoint in the skill now returns a single provenance row. MOSS-Transcribe-Diarize is also new under that rule, but the 2026-10-13 report (#239, still open) already picked it.

All three claim their gain on meetings. Their leaderboard averages tie parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2, while their AMI WER is 1.2 to 2.1 points lower. AMI is in each one's training data, so that AMI figure is not a held-out number, and only the app's own meeting tracks can settle it. Each is also over the 1.5 GB file budget at every published quantisation.

## Granite Speech 4.1 2B NAR

- Role: speech recognition, meetings first. Would replace Parakeet Ultra.
- Claimed numbers: on the Open ASR Leaderboard, AMI-Cleaned WER is 6.96 against 9.09 for parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2, and the average is 6.45 against 6.43, a tie. RTFx is 2,113 against 6,148 on the leaderboard's GPU (2.9× slower). transcribe.cpp measures 75× realtime on an M4 Max (Metal). [Leaderboard CSV](https://huggingface.co/datasets/hf-audio/open-asr-leaderboard-results/blob/main/english_short_latest.csv), [model card](https://huggingface.co/ibm-granite/granite-speech-4.1-2b-nar).
- Licence and size: Apache-2.0, 2.25B parameters. The GGUF Q4_K_M is 1.56 GB ([handy-computer](https://huggingface.co/handy-computer/granite-speech-4.1-2b-nar-gguf)). MLX bf16 and 5-bit (2.4 GB) builds run in mlx-audio. It has no timestamps. For punctuation and capitals, the card points to the autoregressive model below.
- Provenance: published by IBM Research (Hugging Face org `ibm-granite`). Created 2026-03-10, released April 2026, last updated 2026-06-18. It edits a CTC hypothesis in one forward pass instead of decoding token by token ([NLE, arXiv 2603.08397](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08397)). The parts are a 440M Conformer CTC encoder, a 160M Q-Former, and granite-4.0-1b-base made bidirectional and LoRA-adapted. It loads as Transformers remote code; IBM's repo is [ibm-granite/granite-speech-models](https://github.com/ibm-granite/granite-speech-models) (52 stars). 94,292 downloads, 61 likes.
- Used by: [Handy](https://github.com/cjpais/Handy) (34k stars) and its runtime [transcribe.cpp](https://github.com/handy-computer/transcribe.cpp) (2.1k), which is the ggml library the app used before Ultra. Also sokuji (1.4k), WinSTT (34) and HumeAI/asr-benchmark-optimization (12). Community GGUF, ONNX and MLX ports exist.
- Eval to run: `make asr-eval`, added as one file in `Scripts/asr-eval/systems/` that calls the transcribe.cpp CLI. Run the 12 meeting tracks first (shipped: 12.55% after the listen review), since that is where the gain is claimed. Predicted failures: the file budget, punctuation on dictation, and word timings for speaker labels.

## Granite Speech 4.1 2B

- Role: speech recognition. Would replace Parakeet Ultra.
- Claimed numbers: AMI-Cleaned 7.06 against 9.09, and an average of 6.42 against 6.43. RTFx is 547 (11× slower than v2 on the leaderboard GPU). It writes punctuation and capitals when the prompt asks for them, and supports keyword biasing. The 100 h AMI training set is in its data. [Model card](https://huggingface.co/ibm-granite/granite-speech-4.1-2b).
- The Plus variant (`granite-speech-4.1-2b-plus`) adds speaker labels and per-word end times but drops punctuation. Its card reports 14.6% of words under the wrong speaker on AMI-SDM, and timestamps reliable on segments up to 3.5 minutes. [Plus card](https://huggingface.co/ibm-granite/granite-speech-4.1-2b-plus).
- Licence and size: Apache-2.0, 2.31B parameters. IBM's own GGUF is Q4_K_M 1.14 GB plus a 1.16 GB f16 audio projector, 2.3 GB together. It runs in llama.cpp and in transcribe.cpp.
- Provenance: IBM Research. Created 2026-04-16, released 2026-04-29, last updated 2026-06-12. It is a Conformer encoder and Q-Former projector feeding granite-4.0-1b-base, decoded autoregressively. It succeeds granite-4.0-1b-speech at the same parameter count and is in Transformers as `GraniteSpeechForConditionalGeneration`. 187,703 downloads, 168 likes.
- Used by: Handy (34k stars), transcribe.cpp (2.1k), sokuji (1.4k), TTS-Audio-Suite (1.2k), [CrispASR](https://github.com/CrispStrobe/CrispASR) (755), izwi (392), pi-voice (301).
- Eval to run: `make asr-eval` through transcribe.cpp. Autoregressive decoding makes the 300 ms dictation p95 the likely failure, so time the 50 dictation clips before running the meeting tracks.

## Canary-Qwen 2.5B

- Role: speech recognition. Would replace Parakeet Ultra.
- Claimed numbers: AMI-Cleaned 7.91 against 9.09, and an average of 6.37 against 6.43. RTFx is 882 (7.0× slower on the leaderboard GPU). transcribe.cpp measures 42× realtime on an M4 Max (Metal). It is English only, with punctuation and capitals. AMI was oversampled to about 15% of its training data. It was trained on clips up to 40 s, so meetings need shorter chunks than the app uses now. [Model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/canary-qwen-2.5b).
- Licence and size: CC-BY-4.0, the same licence as Parakeet. 2.56B parameters. GGUF Q4_K_M is 1.74 GB ([handy-computer](https://huggingface.co/handy-computer/canary-qwen-2.5b-gguf)), and a Core ML int8 build ([phequals](https://huggingface.co/phequals/canary-qwen-2.5b-coreml-int8)) has about 3.1 GB of weights. It has no timestamps.
- Provenance: NVIDIA NeMo. Created 2025-06-26, last updated 2026-04-21. It is a speech-augmented LLM: the FastConformer encoder from canary-1b-flash, linearly projected into Qwen3-1.7B with LoRA. Code is in [NVIDIA-NeMo/NeMo](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/NeMo) (18.6k stars). 46,276 downloads, 470 likes.
- Used by: Handy (34k stars), transcribe.cpp (2.1k), sokuji (1.4k), pi-voice (301), agent-cli (235), Voconly (145).
- Eval to run: `make asr-eval` through transcribe.cpp, with meeting tracks cut to 40 s chunks or less. It has the smallest claimed gain of the three and the same latency risk as Granite 4.1 2B.

## Near misses

- ibm-granite/granite-4.0-1b-speech: average 7.02 against 6.43. Granite 4.1 2B is its successor at the same size.
- ibm-granite/granite-speech-3.3-2b and 3.3-8b: their averages (5.36, 5.26) leave out URGENT2024. On the shared columns, 3.3-2b trails 4.1 2B (AMI 7.80 against 7.06). 3.0B and 9B parameters.
- microsoft/Phi-4-multimodal-instruct: average 5.02 without URGENT2024, and AMI 9.12. 5.6B parameters.
- Edge0/ARK-ASR-3B: average 6.53, below its 0.6B sibling, with 4.1B parameters.
- Edge0/Audio8-ASR-0.1B and soundsgoodai/Zipformer-cr-ctc-transducer-XL-290M: CC-BY-NC-4.0.
- nvidia/nemotron-3.5-asr-streaming-0.6b: average 10.51; Nemotron ASR is rejected in `model-trials.md`.
- nvidia/parakeet-ctc-1.1b, distil-whisper/distil-large-v3.5, FunAudioLLM/Fun-ASR-Nano-2512, espnet/owsm_ctc_v4_1B, microsoft/VibeVoice-ASR, facebook/omniASR LLM-7B and CTC-7B, the Mistral Voxtral rows and the abr-ai niagara 9m to 84m rows: every average is 6.90 or worse.
- parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2 and v3 "fast-gpu-asr" rows: an NVIDIA GPU runtime for models already seen.
- assemblyai/universal-3-5-pro, smallestai/pulse, sprag/symphony, gladia/solaria-3, modulate/multilingual: proprietary and cloud only, like the cloud rows listed on 2026-10-13.
- mlx-community/Phonon-2: an MLX build of Phonon-2, which is rejected in `model-trials.md`.
- mlx-audio 0.5.9 adds Nemotron speech streaming STT: nemotron-speech-streaming-en-0.6b is already in `seen.json`.
- Clean-up: tencent/Hy-Memory-30B-A3B, inclusionAI/Ling-3.1-flash, Ternary-Bonsai-2 (2-bit, Qwen3.5 base), IFM/K2-Horizon-0.9B and Muse Spark Open publish no clean-up or text-normalisation score, and the large ones do not fit.
- Decision 3.0 (Jev-Style-0.8B-Decision-v3, vllm-sr d3) classifiers: no score for a role we ship a model for.
- sachmatkris/agirdi (Lithuanian), Voxtral-Mini-4B-Realtime-Arabic and the Hebrew code-switching Parakeet v2 GGUF: not English.
