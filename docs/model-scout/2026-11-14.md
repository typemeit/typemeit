## Speech recognition: Fluid Speech Pro (Core ML)

- Role: speech recognition, dictation and meetings. Would replace FluidAudio Parakeet Ultra.
- What it is: a 0.63B English and European speech model from FluidVoice, the team behind the FluidVoice dictation app. It is NVIDIA's Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 (FastConformer encoder, TDT decoder) with one small extra block after the encoder's subsampling, built on both Parakeet v3 and Moondream's Parakeet Ultra. An optional clean mode drops fillers and repeated words (not yet in the Core ML build). The same team's Fluid Speech Mini was rejected on 2026-10-17; Pro is a separate, larger re-train, not a variant of Mini.
- Claimed numbers ([card](https://huggingface.co/FluidVoice-Labs/fluid-speech-pro)), maintainer-run on the Open ASR Leaderboard ([PR 263](https://github.com/huggingface/open_asr_leaderboard/pull/263), rerun by the maintainers): average WER 6.06 against 7.04 for Parakeet TDT v3 and 6.93 for v2; AMI-Cleaned 7.55 against 9.42 (v3) and 9.09 (v2); private conversational 12.80 against 13.53 (v3). Leaderboard numbers use 4-beam search; the Core ML build decodes greedily, which the card puts at about 0.6% more errors. Voice Arena US English (independent, phone calls): rank 16, semantic WER 2.91% against 3.01% for Parakeet-TDT-0.6B, with overlapping intervals. Ultra has no leaderboard row, so the gain over what we ship is unknown. The card names no training data, so whether AMI is held out cannot be checked; treat the AMI gain as unconfirmed.
- Licence and size: CC-BY-4.0. Core ML build 652 MB (int8 encoder, fp16 decoder and joint), about 250 MB peak memory and a 16.6-minute file in 4.2 s on an M5 Max, per the card.
- Provenance: published by Hugging Face org FluidVoice-Labs; the leaderboard PR is from GitHub user altic-dev, maker of [altic-dev/FluidVoice](https://github.com/altic-dev/FluidVoice) (macOS dictation app, GPL-3.0, 13,025 stars, pushed 2026-11-14). Main card created 2026-10-30, updated 2026-11-13; the Core ML, MLX, GGUF and ONNX builds were published 2026-11-13. 8,732 downloads and 22 likes on the main card, 42 and 1 on the Core ML build. Its Swift loader `AsrModels.load(from:version: .fluidParakeetPro)` lives in the altic-dev fork of FluidAudio, not upstream; the card says the inputs and outputs match the `parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3-coreml` package (15 s windows, 8,192-piece vocabulary).
- Who uses it: FluidVoice itself; [Neywa-Labs/mlx-audio](https://github.com/Neywa-Labs/mlx-audio) (8,265 stars), [DevEmperor/DictateKeyboard](https://github.com/DevEmperor/DictateKeyboard) (320) and [TypeWhisper/typewhisper-win](https://github.com/TypeWhisper/typewhisper-win) (244) reference it. Community re-uploads: TalkMate/fluid-speech-pro-coreml and -gguf.
- Eval to run: `make asr-eval` with `--like fluid-ultra`, dictation and meeting tracks, reading S/D/I per track type (Fluid Speech Mini lost words on room tracks). Load the Core ML bundle through `Scripts/asr-eval/fluidasr`'s `orukeet` mode (`AsrModels.loadLocal`, v3 layout) by copying `systems/fluid_orukeet.py`; if the extra block makes the bundle incompatible with upstream FluidAudio 0.18.2, the altic-dev fork's `.fluidParakeetPro` is the fallback. Check dictation p95 against 97 ms.

## Near misses

- salesforce-sovereign-intelligence/SOV-Transcribe-1: Whisper large-v3 encoder plus a Gemma 4 26B-A4B decoder, Apache-2.0, leaderboard average 5.96, AMI 8.17. The smallest build (TypeWhisper/SOV-Transcribe-1-4bit, MLX) is 15.6 GB and needs about 16 GB to run; fails "fits in 24 GB beside the app".
- yoob/yoob-asr-pro-v1 (eval reference): new leaderboard row, maintainer-run, AMI-Cleaned 5.94 and private conversational 11.98, against Zoom Scribe v2.1 Pro's 5.96 / 11.67. Word timings are only on its Turbo model, and the edge proxy closes requests after about 100 s, so it cannot take a 1-hour file in one request with word timestamps.
- sahilmahendrakar/Earlybird-29M: a 4-bit Moonshine Tiny fine-tune that predicts words before they end; 12.6% mean WER on the Open ASR sets, far behind Ultra.
- oruk/orukqwen-asr-1.7b and -v2: RL post-trains of Qwen3-ASR-1.7B (AMI 7.92 / 7.68); same architecture as Qwen3-ASR, rejected 2026-10-12, and 4.1 GB, over the file budget.
- Fish Audio Transcribe 1 Pro (eval reference): Voice Arena US English rank 11, behind Universal-3 Pro at rank 3.
- swaynehales/granite-speech-5.0-470m-turboctc Core ML and MLX ports: Granite 5.0 470M was rejected 2026-09-30; ports with no new numbers.
- xix3r/voxix-* GGUF ports (Parakeet, Moonshine, Whisper, Nemotron), xiaoxiaojian/nemotron-3.5-asr-0.6b-325mb-gguf, Benaeo/parakeet-unified-en-0.6b-ggml, AnyAutomation and Aavola Parakeet v3 ONNX, Mer0vin8ian/LES-eg2-665M-asr-en: ports of models already tried or seen, no new numbers.
- kramerthomas/edda-v0.2-coreml, hynt/MOSS-Transcribe-Diarize-Vietnamese-6000h: not English.
- arXiv 2611.16371 (structured speaker conditioning for target-speaker ASR): a method on Qwen3-ASR-0.6B, no released weights named.
