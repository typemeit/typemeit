## Fluid Speech Mini (Core ML)

- Role: speech recognition. Would replace Parakeet Ultra.
- What it is: FluidVoice's dictation model. It fine-tunes NVIDIA's Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 encoder for dictation and reuses Parakeet Ultra's decoder and joint network. The weights are stored at about 3 bits each, and the model was trained to keep transcribing the main speaker while someone else talks nearby. Published 2026-10-13.
- Claimed numbers: an average WER of 4.54 against 4.56 for Parakeet Ultra over 8 public Open ASR Leaderboard sets (AMI 8.34). WER with another voice in the room (Google SVQ, background speech) is 10.9 against Ultra's 14.3. These are self-reported runs of the leaderboard's code; the model has no public leaderboard row. The card says larger Parakeet models do a couple of points better in traffic noise. Its training data is not listed. [Model card](https://huggingface.co/FluidVoice-Labs/fluid-speech-mini-coreml).
- Licence and size: CC-BY-4.0, with credit to "Fluid Speech Mini by FluidVoice". The Core ML package is 253 MB against Ultra's 613 MB, uses about 277 MB of memory and runs on the Neural Engine at 25 ms per 15-second window. ONNX (302 MB) and GGUF (347 MB) builds exist.
- Provenance: FluidVoice (the company behind [altic-dev/FluidVoice](https://github.com/altic-dev/FluidVoice), 12.2k stars, a macOS dictation app). Created and last updated on Hugging Face 2026-10-13. The audio preprocessor comes from FluidInference's Parakeet v3 Core ML package. The card's Swift example uses a FluidAudio fork, altic-dev/FluidAudio, but says upstream FluidAudio loads it as a Parakeet v3 model. Parakeet Ultra itself was published by Moondream (`moondream/parakeet-ultra`, 2026-09-22) and packaged by FluidAudio. 128 downloads, 5 likes.
- Used by: FluidVoice's own apps. Code search finds watzon/sayso (34 stars) and two repos with fewer than 5 stars.
- Eval to run: `make asr-eval`. It needs a `systems/` file and a load-from-folder option in `Scripts/asr-eval/fluidasr`, which today only takes the `ultra` and `phonon2` downloads (`AsrModels.load(from:version: .v3)`). Run the room and others tracks first. Training it to follow one speaker may drop the other people in a room recording, and that would make it a dictation-only model, which the one-model rule forbids.

## Eval reference: Zoom Scribe v2 Pro (meetings)

- Role: eval reference for meetings. Would replace AssemblyAI Universal-3.5 Pro.
- What it is: Zoom's paid speech-to-text API, the "Pro" tier of Scribe v2, called through Zoom's AI Services API.
- Claimed numbers, all maintainer-run on the Open ASR Leaderboard (PR #210, confirmed by Zoom 2026-09-04): AMI-Cleaned 6.08 against 10.63 for Universal-3.5 Pro, and private conversational 12.10 against 12.80. Live average 4.70 against 5.39. Artificial Analysis gives AA-WER 2.8 against 3.1 for Universal-3 Pro.
- Price and model id: $0.20 an hour synchronous, $0.15 batch. On 2026-10-15 Zoom added a documented `model` request field to `POST /v2/aiservices/scribe/transcribe` and `/scribe/jobs`; the leaderboard's harness requests `zoom-scribe-en-pro`. A request carries at most 100 MB and 2 hours, so a 1-hour track goes as FLAC or MP3.
- Provenance: Zoom Video Communications. Scribe v1 joined the leaderboard 2026-04-09, v2 Pro on 2026-09-04. Speaker labels (`config.diarization`) and word timestamps (`word_time_offsets`) are documented. No source publishes how accurate its speaker labels are.
- Used by: the leaderboard's own harness ([zoom_provider.py](https://github.com/huggingface/open_asr_leaderboard/blob/main/api/providers/zoom_provider.py)).
- Eval to run: transcribe the 12 meeting tracks, then the user judges the spans where it and Universal-3.5 Pro disagree (`listen-review` skill).

## Eval reference: ElevenLabs Scribe v2 (dictations)

- Role: eval reference for dictations, and the fallback for meetings. Would replace AssemblyAI Universal-3.5 Pro.
- What it is: ElevenLabs' paid speech-to-text API (`scribe_v2`).
- Claimed numbers: Artificial Analysis gives AA-WER 2.2, with 1.5 on its voice-agent clips, the closest thing it has to dictation; Universal-3 Pro scores 3.1 and 2.1. Long-form average is 7.32 against 8.34 for Universal-3 Pro (`longform_latest.csv`). Its leaderboard AMI-Cleaned is weaker: 8.19 against Zoom's 6.08.
- Price and model id: $0.22 an hour, plus $0.05 with keyterms. Files up to 10 hours, speaker labels for up to 32 speakers, and word timestamps by default.
- Provenance: ElevenLabs. Its changelog has no speech-to-text entry since 2026-10-10.
- Eval to run: transcribe the 50 dictations, then listen-review against Universal-3.5 Pro.
