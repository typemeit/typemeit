## Eval reference, speaker labels: Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe (cloud, DeepInfra)

- Role: eval reference (paid, cloud), for the speaker labels on meetings. Would replace the speaker labels from AssemblyAI Universal-3.5 Pro, which AssemblyAI deprecated on 2026-11-17. It should not replace AssemblyAI for the words (numbers below).
- What it is: NVIDIA's speech language model, created on Hugging Face 2026-10-07. A speech recognition encoder and the Nemotron 3 Diarization speaker encoder feed a 30B mixture-of-experts language model, which writes the transcript with a speaker tag on each turn. The weights were seen 2026-10-29 and are too big to ship (67.5 GB). It comes back now for a different role, because two independent boards rank its speaker labels and DeepInfra sells it as an API.
- Claimed numbers:
  - Artificial Analysis Diarization Index ([leaderboard](https://artificialanalysis.ai/speech-to-text/non-streaming), [method](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/speech-to-text), independent, 97 conversations, 6.8 hours, 2 to 8 people): rank 1 at 3.3% of words under the wrong speaker or missed, against 8.0% for AssemblyAI STT-3.7 (picked 2026-11-18) and 8.9% for Universal-3.5 Pro. On Artificial Analysis' own held-out conversations: 2.0% against 6.5% and 7.2%. Conversations run 2 to 9 minutes, shorter than our meetings, and some of the speech is synthetic.
  - Voice Arena Diarization Bench, Diarization + ASR, English ([leaderboard](https://voicearena.com/diarization-bench), independent, 139 sessions): rank 2, cpWER 32.43% (95% CI 26.93 to 38.03) against 47.81% for AssemblyAI Universal-3.6 Pro at rank 9 and 23.89% for AirCaps A5S Async v1 at rank 1 (picked 2026-11-15). cpWER counts a word under the wrong speaker as an error.
  - Words only, where it does not lead: Artificial Analysis WER 3.42% against 2.51% for STT-3.7 and 2.99% for Universal-3.5 Pro; Open ASR private conversational 13.01% against 12.62% for Universal-3.5 Pro. Its AMI-Cleaned 7.78% against 10.63% is not held out.
  - Training data: AMI, ICSI, AliMeeting, AISHELL-4, DiPCo and Fisher are in it ([card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Transcribe-30B-A4B)). NOTSOFAR-1 is listed for evaluation only. Artificial Analysis' own conversations are proprietary.
- Shared errors: its speaker encoder is Nemotron 3 Diarization, the model the app ships. A reference built on it can make the same mistakes as the shipped diarizer and hide them, so it would make the shipped diarizer look better than it is and would not be a fair judge between it and another diarizer. Use it as a second opinion where it and AssemblyAI disagree, decided by ear.
- Price and model id: $0.002 per minute ($0.12 per audio hour) on DeepInfra; model id `nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Transcribe-30B-A4B`, with `diarize=true` and `chunk_level=word`. Each word comes back with a start, an end and a speaker that stays the same across the recording ([schema](https://api.deepinfra.com/models/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Transcribe-30B-A4B/schema/default)). Recordings up to one hour. No file size limit is published, and the schema lists a chunk length of at most 30 seconds, so check the first long file. A meeting over one hour has to be cut.
- Which endpoint was scored: Artificial Analysis does not say on the diarization chart. Its WER table lists this model through DeepInfra, but its method page says third-party-hosted models are not in the diarization version.
- Data use: DeepInfra says inputs and outputs are held in memory only and are not trained on ([privacy](https://docs.deepinfra.com/account/data-privacy)).
- Licence of the weights: OpenMDW-1.1. The API is a paid service, so size and licence budgets do not apply.
- Provenance: NVIDIA. Created 2026-10-07, last updated 2026-10-29; 96,651 downloads and 2,427 likes on Hugging Face. Built on `NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B`; code in NVIDIA-NeMo/Speech.
- Who uses it: `gh search code` finds it in huggingface/transformers (169,300 stars), vllm-project/vllm (97,533), NVIDIA-NeMo/Speech (18,787), modelscope/ms-swift (16,168), huggingface/speech-to-speech (13,785), Neywa-Labs/mlx-audio (8,330), 0xShug0/audio.cpp (4,451) and FluidInference/FluidAudio (3,331).
- Eval to run: add a `deepinfra.py` beside `Scripts/asr-eval/assemblyai.py`, run it on the 12 meeting tracks, and compare its labels with AssemblyAI's on the wrong-speaker word rate (`make asr-eval ARGS=score`). The user then judges the stretches where the two give the same words to different people (`listen-review` skill). Run it in the same round as STT-3.7 and A5S Async v1, which are both picked and untried.
